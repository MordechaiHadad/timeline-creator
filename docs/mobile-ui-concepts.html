Timeline Creator

Small screen concepts

The chosen direction: two views, one editor, drawn at 380 × 800. Canvas is for comparing dates, Agenda is for reading, and every edit, event or period, uses the same sheet. Tap dots and rows, open the sheets, and switch themes.
